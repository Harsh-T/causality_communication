# A Causal Inference Institute in Communication Studies: A Half-Day Pre-conference

#### **Dates**: Thursday, 4 June | 1:00 PM \- 5:00 PM (Half-Day)

#### **Venue**: Waterfront (*Walking distance from the Main ICA Hotel)*

This pre-conference builds on the momentum of the successful 2025 Blue Sky Workshop on causal inference. The field of communications has demonstrated a clear demand for more structured and sustained engagement with this topic, and our goal is to meet that need by establishing a cross-disciplinary community of scholars and keeping the conversation going. This event will move beyond introductory concepts to address practical applications and facilitate advanced discussions, with a focus on showcasing emerging scholarship.

## 2:00pm \- 2:45pm Workshop:

Tian Yang, Promise and Perils of Quasi-Experimental Design: The Example of Regression Discontinuity Design

*This workshop discusses quasi-experimental designs, positioning them as important tools for elevating communication theory through rigorous causal inference. We will first examine how these designs offer new avenues for theoretical advancement. The session then pivots to a practical deep dive into Regression Discontinuity Design (RDD), where we will navigate the specific empirical challenges while discussing potential strategies for its implementation in communication research.*

Tian Yang is an assistant professor at the School of Journalism and Communication, the Chinese University of Hong Kong. His research lies at the intersection between digital media, political communication, and computational social science, which particularly looks at people’s information behaviors in the current new media environment across the globe.
