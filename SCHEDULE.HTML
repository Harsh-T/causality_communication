A Causal Inference Institute in Communication Studies

This pre-conference builds on the momentum of the successful 2025 Blue Sky Workshop on causal inference. This event will move beyond introductory concepts to address practical applications and facilitate advanced discussions, with a focus on showcasing emerging scholarship.
